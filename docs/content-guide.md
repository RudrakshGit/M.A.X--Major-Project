# Writing resources and journeys

Content lives in `src/content/` as typed data, not prose files, so it is
translatable and testable.

## Sourcing

Ground every technique in a public, citable source — NHS, NIMH, WHO,
Tele-MANAS, or a peer-reviewed CBT/CBT-I text. Put the source in the content
record. If you cannot cite it, do not ship it.

Never copy a copyrighted questionnaire or a paywalled worksheet. See
[`research.md`](research.md) §6 for which instruments we may use.

## A resource

Short. One idea. Read in under three minutes.

Structure: what this feeling is → why it happens, briefly and without jargon →
one thing to try now → when to talk to a person. That last section is not
optional; every resource routes back to a human.

Write for a student who is tired and not in the mood to read. Front-load the
useful part. No preamble about how common anxiety is.

## A journey

A short multi-day track for one condition. Five to seven days, one small step
per day, under ten minutes each.

Each day: a short read, one exercise, one reflection prompt. Days build on each
other but any day must stand alone, because students miss days and must not
feel they have failed. Missing a day is normal — never guilt the student on
return.

## Exercises we ship

Box breathing · 5-4-3-2-1 grounding · thought record and reframe · worry time ·
behavioural activation · sleep wind-down and stimulus control · self-compassion
break. All CBT or CBT-I derived and freely describable.

## Tone rules

- Never promise an outcome. "This helps some people" beats "This will fix it".
- Never imply effort is the missing ingredient.
- No streaks, no badges, no "you broke your chain". Gamified guilt is harmful
  in this domain and we do not ship it.

## Checklist before content ships

Source cited · reading time under the cap · routes to
a human at the end · no diagnosis language · no copyrighted instrument.

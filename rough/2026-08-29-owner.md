## Decisions taken since

- Build is **solo** — Rudraksh implements everything. Roles are assigned at
  presentation time by the owner, not by this repository. Do not reintroduce a
  track split.
- The campus dashboard demo uses **real data from our own college**, which
  makes onboarding ten or more real students a Phase 04 deliverable.
- Name stays **MAX**. The expansion is the owner's to change at any time; the
  repository uses "Mental-health Access & eXpression" until told otherwise.
- Owner, 2026-08-30: "Hindi + English drop kr diya tha sirf english rahega."
  The interface is English only. Bilingual UI is out of scope and brief 012 was
  deleted rather than deferred.

  Kept deliberately: the safety classifier's Hindi and Hinglish patterns. A
  student in distress writes in the language they think in regardless of what
  language the buttons are in, so input detection and interface language are
  separate concerns. Those patterns are load-bearing — do not remove them as
  dead code.

  Known trade-off for the viva: the problem statement names accessibility in
  rural and semi-urban colleges, and an English-only interface is a weaker
  answer to that. The remaining accessibility answer is the performance budget,
  the PWA and the pseudonymous, stigma-free design.
